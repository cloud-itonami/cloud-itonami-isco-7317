# Business Model: Wood & Basketry Handicraft Workshop Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-7317`
- ISCO-08: `7317`
- Occupation: Handicraft Workers in Wood, Basketry and Related Materials
- Social impact: worker-safety, craft-heritage-continuity, artisan-livelihood-continuity

## Customer

- wood-and-basketry handicraft workshop owners / artisans
- independent craft cooperatives / craft-workshop collectives

## Offer

- crew shift/task scheduling coordination
- task/materials-usage/progress-record logging
- wood/basketry-materials supply-order coordination
- safety-concern surfacing to workshop safety officers

## Revenue

- monthly retainer
- per-workshop coordination fee

## Trust Controls

- no direct finalization of a craft-fabrication-execution decision
  (e.g. a specific carving, weaving or finishing step), ever
- no override of a workshop safety officer's judgment, ever
- flagged safety concerns (cut hazards, dust exposure,
  repetitive-strain injury) always route to human sign-off, regardless
  of confidence
- no supply order above the registered cost threshold without
  governor-gated human sign-off
- operating and coordination records are auditable, not editable
